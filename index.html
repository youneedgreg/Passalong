Passalong

Turn a worksheet into a tiny file that works offline on any phone.

  1. 1 Make Write or paste a worksheet below.
  2. 2 Practise Open it as a learner, or send the file. It works with no internet.
  3. 3 Collect Paste the codes learners send back: open Collect.

1 Make

One line per part. The format is under "How to write a worksheet" below.

    Open as a learner (opens a new tab)

    File: … · Link: … · Code: …

    How to write a worksheet

    Start each line with one of these. A line that starts with anything else is joined to the line above. A blank line ends that.

    Title:
    The worksheet title. Required, 80 characters at most.
    Subject: Level: Intro:
    Shown under the title. Optional.
    Lang:
    Language code for screen readers, such as en or sw. Defaults to en.
    Teacher-SMS:
    The number "Send by SMS" goes to. Optional.
    Q:
    Starts a question. 1 to 20 questions.
    *
    The correct option. Exactly one per question.
    -
    A wrong option. Text after | is the feedback for that mistake.
    =
    An accepted typed answer, 1 to 5 per question. Don't mix with options.
    Hint: Why:
    The hint and the worked answer. Optional.
